# Matrix Regional Route Design

## 1. Objective

Complete one evidence-led, end-to-end preparation cycle for six priority public companies in countries near China or with strong China trade links. For each company, research the official site, identify reusable public-site content gaps, improve Huasheng's localized buyer journey, verify desktop and mobile usability, and prepare a review-only first-contact draft. This project does not authorize external sending or production deployment.

## 2. Fixed First Cohort

The first cohort is fixed for this cycle:

1. Mit Mongkol Industry Co., Ltd. — Thailand — personal care and household liquid refill or spouted pouches.
2. Lix Detergent Joint Stock Company — Vietnam — detergent, softener, dishwashing-liquid refill pouches and powder bags.
3. JD Food Public Company Limited — Thailand — seasoning packets, sauce pouches and snack pouches.
4. Cholimex Food Joint Stock Company — Vietnam — sauce sachets, liquid pouches and spouted formats.
5. Orda Trade Astana LLP — Kazakhstan — tea pouches, tea-bag overwrap and machine-compatible roll film.
6. PT Adev Natural Indonesia — Indonesia — personal-care OEM, multi-SKU liquid packaging and refill pouches.

India remains excluded. EU prospects remain deferred. A company may be marked blocked after research, but it must not be silently replaced in this cycle; replacement requires an explicit cohort revision.

## 3. Ranking Policy

Candidate priority is determined in this order:

1. geographic proximity to China and practical trade links;
2. demonstrated manufacturing, OEM/private-label, export, capacity or multi-SKU activity;
3. fit with Huasheng's evidenced production capability;
4. official-source coverage and organizational contact accessibility;
5. reusable localized-site value for similar companies in the same country and segment.

The database fit score is a secondary input and cannot override geographic policy or evidence blockers.

## 4. Research Dossier

Each company requires a dated dossier stored in the authoritative management project. The dossier must inspect, when available:

- official home and company profile;
- products, portfolio and package formats;
- services, OEM/ODM, development and production workflow;
- quality, testing, certification and regulatory pages;
- sustainability and material-priority pages;
- official contact, sourcing, export or procurement route.

Every material fact records its source URL and one of `confirmed`, `inferred`, `unknown`, or `not_relevant`. Inferences remain internal. The dossier records operating model, scale, SKU complexity, product and packaging forms, likely workflow concerns, official organizational contact routes, supplier relationship confidence, and questions that belong in first contact.

Fewer than three distinct official sources, no company/profile evidence, no product evidence, no development/service-process evidence, or no official organizational contact route blocks drafting.

## 5. Reusable Content-Gap Mapping

Every confirmed concern maps to exactly one outcome:

- `existing_ready`: an existing localized page answers it;
- `public_gap`: add reusable country- or segment-level content;
- `internal_note`: useful for drafting but unsuitable for publication;
- `blocked`: Huasheng lacks evidence or operational support for the claim.

Public pages must never name a prospect. Content must remain useful to comparable buyers and may cover:

- one representative SKU or current sample as the first review step;
- formula, filling condition and fitment inputs for liquid products;
- machine direction, web width, repeat length and sealing-window inputs for roll film;
- moisture, aroma, oil and seal-control questions for food products;
- multi-SKU artwork, barcode and repeat-order controls;
- representative testing and conditional structure selection;
- delivery to a designated China warehouse or coordination for factory inspection where applicable.

The site must not promise a final material structure, barrier result, leak result, lead time, price, universal MOQ, certification or test result without current evidence.

## 6. Localized Buyer Journeys

Each company maps to the smallest coherent reusable route set:

- localized home;
- localized about page;
- localized market page;
- one localized application page;
- one or two localized product pages.

Required language families for this cohort are Thai, Vietnamese, Indonesian, and Russian or an existing appropriate Central Asian locale. English remains available as a fallback, but an untranslated English catalog is not a localized journey.

Each route set uses the application page as the primary first-contact link and the localized about page as the optional secondary credibility link. The first message must not contain a list of all available URLs.

## 7. Website Readiness Gate

A route set may become `ready` only after all of the following pass:

1. build, type-check and project validation;
2. canonical localized URLs return HTTP 200 and expose correct canonical and hreflang values;
3. visible fallback audit has no P0 or P1 English leakage;
4. desktop and mobile headings, cards, links and calls to action are usable with no horizontal overflow;
5. every proposed first-contact URL is verified on the deployed public site;
6. the verification record stores route-set ID, locale, category, source commit or release, checks, timestamp and result.

Any content change, failed health check or deployment change returns the route set to `stale` until reverified. Deployment and service restart require explicit approval.

## 8. Strategy-Match Gate

Initial-draft creation requires a deterministic score of at least 75/100 and no critical blocker. Components are:

- official-source role coverage: 24;
- operating model and scale: 15;
- product/category and format alignment: 20;
- development, packaging, quality or regulatory workflow: 15;
- evidenced buyer priorities: 10;
- official organizational access: 8;
- verified localized buyer journey: 8.

The score is recalculated from current authoritative evidence and live route readiness. Text-quality score is displayed separately and cannot substitute for strategy match. Legacy drafts and drafts whose evidence changed are invalidated.

## 9. First-Contact Draft Contract

Only a ready route set and passing strategy-match gate may produce a draft. Each draft contains:

1. an evidence-backed reason for contacting the company;
2. one concrete packaging scenario;
3. a low-friction next step based on one representative SKU, existing sample, photo or current specification;
4. a concise Huasheng introduction;
5. one primary localized application link and at most one localized about link;
6. one clear, answerable question;
7. `Gavin` and `Huasheng Printing Co., Ltd.` signature;
8. one brief courtesy closing in the target country's language;
9. a complete Chinese translation for internal review.

The English body is the authoritative outbound version. The local-language text is limited to the courtesy closing unless a later explicit decision approves a full bilingual message. Card and final preview preserve greeting, paragraph, question, links, courtesy closing and signature as separate lines. No sentence may be truncated with an ellipsis in the review display.

Drafts are review-only. No email, WhatsApp message, website form submission or formal publication occurs without the existing explicit approval gates.

## 10. Execution Order

Companies are processed sequentially to prevent research and content assumptions from contaminating another record:

1. Mit Mongkol;
2. Lix Detergent;
3. JD Food;
4. Cholimex Food;
5. Orda Trade Astana;
6. PT Adev Natural Indonesia.

For each company: dossier → evidence review → content-gap map → reusable route changes → local verification → deployment approval → production verification → strategy score → draft review.

Shared content may be reused by later companies only when its facts and claims remain valid for the later segment. Company-specific facts remain in the management project and never enter public reusable copy.

## 11. Data and Security Boundaries

- Use only public organizational information and official or authoritative public sources.
- Do not bypass logins, CAPTCHA or access controls; do not infer private contacts.
- Store customer-specific evidence only in the authoritative management project.
- Do not copy credentials, tokens, cookies, message identifiers or actual business records into user-level catalogs or public-site content.
- Preserve neutral technical codenames for private capabilities and workflows.
- Preserve separate approval for production deployment and every external send.

## 12. Acceptance Criteria

The cycle is complete when:

- all six dossiers have dated source matrices and confidence classifications;
- each company has a reusable content-gap decision and route-set status;
- all implemented localized routes pass local desktop and mobile verification;
- approved production deployment, if authorized, passes public HTTP, canonical, hreflang and responsive smoke checks;
- every company either has a strategy score of at least 75 with no blocker and a formatted review-only draft, or has an explicit blocker with the next evidence/content action;
- no prospect name appears in public website content;
- no outbound message is sent during this cycle.

## 13. Rollback

Website changes remain independently reversible by commit or release. A failed route or production check blocks drafts and restores the prior active website release according to the existing `site-runtime` procedure. Management records retain audit history; stale drafts are superseded rather than deleted.
