# Matrix Signal Sprint Design

**Date:** 2026-07-17  
**Target domain:** `https://gdhspack.com`  
**Sprint window:** 2026-07-17 through 2026-07-24  
**Public outreach identity:** `Huasheng Packaging Editorial Team <sales@gdhspack.com>`

## 1. Objective

Run a one-week, zero-budget authority-building sprint for `gdhspack.com`. Complete 8 individually reviewed, relevant submissions with evidence, retain 3 strictly qualified reserve targets, and seek 3–7 links that become publicly available during the sprint. The longer-term outcome remains 10–15 valid referring root domains, but third-party review and publication timing are outside the project's control. The 8-submission and 3-reserve scope was approved by the user after strict screening found that the original 15-submission target could not be met without lowering the evidence gate.

The sprint excludes paid placements, private blog networks, expired-domain redirects, automated link creation, bulk outreach, hidden links, and arrangements whose primary condition is reciprocal linking.

## 2. Canonical Public Identity

All profiles, biographies, directory entries, and pitches use the same public facts:

- Brand: Huasheng Packaging
- Legal entity: Chaozhou Chao'an Huasheng Printing Co., Ltd.
- Address: Longshewei, Dongmei Road, Chao'an District, Chaozhou, Guangdong, China
- Website: `https://gdhspack.com`
- Outreach identity: Huasheng Packaging Editorial Team
- Email: `sales@gdhspack.com`

Before any submission, conflicting company names, Shantou addresses, or inconsistent production claims on the target site must be identified. A submission that depends on a disputed fact remains blocked until that fact is confirmed.

## 3. One-Week Deliverables

By 2026-07-24, the sprint will produce:

1. A baseline of currently discoverable referring domains and target-site linkable assets.
2. A reviewed pool of 30–40 candidate sites, including at least 11 strictly qualified targets.
3. 8 qualified submissions and 3 qualified reserve targets across the approved source mix.
4. Submission evidence and status for every attempted target.
5. Verification records for every link that becomes public.
6. A pending queue with the next follow-up date for third-party reviews that outlast the sprint.
7. A final report separating live, pending, rejected, blocked, and disqualified targets.

## 4. Source Mix

The intended longer-term mix is:

| Source class | Target referring domains | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Reviewed free directories and company profiles | 4–5 | Establish consistent entity references |
| Editorial contributions and expert articles | 3–4 | Earn relevant editorial citations |
| Resource pages, broken-link replacements, and content citations | 2–3 | Place useful guides where readers need them |
| Existing mentions and genuine business relationships | 1–3 | Convert real-world recognition into accurate citations |

The mix is a portfolio target, not a quota that permits low-quality substitutions. A source is rejected if it fails the quality gate even when this reduces the count.

## 5. Candidate Quality Gate

A candidate proceeds only when all mandatory checks pass:

- The site has a real audience or organizational purpose connected to flexible packaging, food manufacturing, printing, films and materials, converting machinery, Guangdong manufacturing, or a closely adjacent field.
- The target page is publicly accessible and has a reasonable path to discovery by search engines.
- The site shows editorial ownership, a legitimate organization, or a maintained company directory.
- The proposed placement helps the source site's readers and is not created solely to manipulate rankings.
- The site does not visibly sell followed links, publish unrelated bulk guest posts, operate as a bookmark farm, or participate in an obvious private network.
- The source does not require payment, goods, services, or a reciprocal followed link in exchange for ranking credit.
- Contact information comes from the organization's public website or another official public source; no private contact data is guessed or collected.

Domain-rating metrics may be recorded as secondary context but never override topical relevance, editorial quality, or the checks above.

## 6. Target Page and Anchor Policy

Expected landing-page distribution across acquired links:

- 40–50%: useful guides, material references, option libraries, or other educational assets.
- 30–40%: homepage or About page for entity citations.
- 15–20%: specific product or application pages when directly relevant to the source content.

Preferred anchors are brand-led or naturally descriptive:

- `Huasheng Packaging`
- `Chaozhou Chao'an Huasheng Printing Co., Ltd.`
- `gdhspack.com` or the full destination URL
- A natural description selected by the publisher, such as a flexible-packaging material guide

The sprint does not request repeated exact-match commercial anchors. Publishers retain editorial control over wording and link attributes.

## 7. Outreach and Submission Workflow

Each candidate moves through the same state flow:

`discovered -> screened -> drafted -> user_review -> submitted -> pending -> live | rejected | disqualified | blocked`

For each target:

1. Record the official source URL and public submission or contact path.
2. Apply the quality gate and document the rationale.
3. Select one relevant destination page and a reader-focused placement reason.
4. Create a customized form response or email draft.
5. Present the exact outbound content to the user for final approval.
6. Submit only after approval.
7. Save non-sensitive evidence of submission and record the timestamp.
8. Follow up at most once when the site's stated process permits it.
9. Verify and record the result if it becomes public.

There is no bulk send. Login and CAPTCHA steps require user takeover. The workflow does not bypass access controls or automate prohibited interactions.

## 8. Content Strategy

Existing product, application, material, option-library, and guide pages are the first linkable assets. Pitches must match an existing page to a source reader's concrete need. Examples include artwork preparation, pouch and roll-film selection, material-structure comparisons, coffee-valve selection, spout-pouch considerations, and food-packaging specification checklists.

Editorial contributions must be original for the accepting publication. They should teach a complete concept without requiring a click to the company site. Any link is a supporting citation or author reference, not the purpose of an otherwise low-value article.

If the candidate pool reveals a repeated unmet need, one narrowly scoped linkable asset may be proposed separately. It is not automatically added to this sprint because website changes require their own approved implementation plan.

## 9. Tracking Record

Each candidate record contains:

- Root domain and source URL
- Source class and topical category
- Evidence of legitimacy and quality-gate result
- Public contact or submission URL
- Selected destination URL
- Proposed placement and anchor context
- Draft text and user-approval status
- Submission timestamp and evidence reference
- Current state and rejection or block reason
- Live source URL and observed link attribute
- Verification timestamp
- One permitted follow-up date

No passwords, session tokens, CAPTCHA data, or private personal data are stored in the tracker.

## 10. Verification and Acceptance

A live referring domain counts toward the result only when:

- The source page is publicly reachable.
- A visible, clickable link resolves to the intended `gdhspack.com` destination without an unexpected redirect.
- The placement is relevant to the surrounding content.
- The source root domain has not already been counted.
- The source still passes the quality gate at verification time.
- The observed `rel` attribute is recorded as followed, `nofollow`, `sponsored`, or `ugc` rather than inferred.

`nofollow`, `sponsored`, and `ugc` links may provide legitimate discovery or brand value and remain in the report, but they are labeled separately. Google Search Console is supporting evidence only because its Links report is a sampled view and may lag publication.

One-week sprint acceptance requires 8 qualified submissions with evidence and 3 strictly qualified reserve targets. The 3–7 live-link range is an execution target, not a guaranteed acceptance gate, because publication is controlled by third parties.

## 11. Failure and Safety Handling

- **Payment or forced exchange requested:** disqualify the target and replace it from the candidate reserve.
- **Publisher requests a qualified paid link:** only consider it for advertising value, outside this zero-budget sprint, and require an appropriate link attribute.
- **Submission rejected:** record the reason; revise and repitch only when the work remains original and the new publication is relevant.
- **No response within the sprint:** keep as pending and assign one follow-up date; do not count it as live.
- **Login or CAPTCHA encountered:** pause for user takeover.
- **Conflicting company fact encountered:** block submission until the user confirms the fact.
- **Source quality deteriorates:** remove it from valid results and draw a replacement candidate.
- **Link disappears or destination breaks:** mark it lost and investigate before claiming it in the final total.

| 7 | Re-verify live links and deliver live, pending, rejected, blocked, and disqualified reports |

## 13. Compliance Basis

The design follows Google's published link-spam guidance. It rejects purchased ranking links, excessive reciprocal-link arrangements, automated link creation, low-quality directory links, optimized anchor text distributed through guest posts or press releases, and low-value content created primarily for ranking manipulation. Legitimate sponsored placements, if ever considered outside this sprint, must use an appropriate qualified link attribute.

## 14. Explicit Non-Goals

- No guarantee of ranking changes, traffic, lead volume, or third-party publication timing.
- No PBN, expired-domain acquisition, bulk directory blasts, forum-signature campaigns, or automated profile creation.
- No public-relations wire distribution or association membership requiring payment.
- No outreach to guessed private email addresses.
- No external communication without the user's final review of the exact message or form content.
